FORSAKEN (Roblox)

FORSAKEN is an asymmetrical survival horror game on Roblox created by the Forsaken Developer Cast. Inspired by games like Dead by Daylight, it pits a group of Survivors against a single Killer in a high-stakes, objective-driven chase.

🎮 How the Game Works

Survivors

Work together across dark, atmospheric maps to solve puzzles, complete objectives, and evade the Killer until time expires.

The Killer

Tracks, hunts, and eliminates all Survivors before time expires using unique abilities to block routes, trap players, and overpower groups.

🔑 Key Features

  • Diverse Characters: Features a variety of Roblox-inspired icons and original characters, each equipped with distinct stats and abilities.
  • Lore & Cosmetics: Includes deep character lore tied to "The Spectre," along with unlockable skins, emotes, and custom audio/chase themes.
  • Spin-Offs & Community Games: Popularity has spawned community spin-offs such as Forsaken Combat, Forsaken RP, and battleground modes.
