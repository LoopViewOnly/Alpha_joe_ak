02 // ADVANCED STRATEGIES

Pro tips and tactical techniques for surviving every encounter.

01

Looping Fundamentals

Never run in straight lines across open areas. Hug walls tightly during window vaults to minimize travel distance and force the Killer to take wider turns.

02

Managing Scratch Marks

Sprinting leaves glowing trails visible to Killers for several seconds. Stop sprinting and crouch-walk behind obstacles to confuse your tracker.

03

Pallet Mindgames

Do not pre-drop every pallet immediately. Stand near pallets to bait the Killer into swinging early, then drop it for a clean stun and distance gain.

04

Stamina & Ability Timing

Keep track of cooldown timers. Triggering escape abilities prematurely leaves you vulnerable during endgame gate rushes.

⚠ TACTICAL REMINDER

Every ability has a purpose. Every pallet is a resource. Every second matters. Think ahead, manage your cooldowns, and never waste an opportunity to create distance.

03 // MAP INTELLIGENCE

Learn the terrain and use environmental advantages to survive.

🏭 THE ABANDONED FACILITY

The Abandoned Facility

Hazards: Tight indoor corridors, stairwells, and echo reverb.

Optimal Strategy: Use multi-level drop-downs to break line of sight. Listen for audio echoes to track Killer verticality.

🌲 THE WHISPERING WOODS

The Whispering Woods

Hazards: Low visibility fog, scattered cabins, and open clearing fields.

Optimal Strategy: Stick close to tree clusters when traversing. Cabin windows provide potentially powerful loop opportunities when timed correctly.
